# Advancing Global AMF Biogeography and Diversity with Long-Read Sequencing and Open Tools

- Our Mission

Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF) are critical mutualists for most terrestrial plants. AMF are
functionally diverse with individual species varying in their impacts on nutrient cycling, plant
diversity, and ecosystem resilience. However, global AMF diversity and biogeography remains
poorly resolved due to limitations in molecular identification and database resources. This
working group will tackle this challenge by leveraging novel long-read sequencing from pure
AMF spore cultures and global environmental datasets to achieve three major aims: (1) develop
the first curated long-read AMF database, (2) build robust, open-access phylogenetic pipelines for
AMF identification across major gene regions; and (3) apply these novel tools to reassess global
distribution patterns of AMF. Our collaborative, interdisciplinary effort will produce key
resources and tools for the broader research community, while fostering data science training and
open science practices.
